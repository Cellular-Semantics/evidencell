# Oriens-Lacunosum Moleculare (O-LM) interneuron — WMBv1 Mapping Report

## Introduction

Oriens-Lacunosum Moleculare (O-LM) cells are a canonical class of CA1 hippocampal GABAergic interneurons defined by a stereotyped geometry: horizontally oriented, spiny dendrites confined to stratum oriens (SO), with an axon that ramifies specifically in stratum lacunosum-moleculare (SLM) onto the apical tufts of CA1 pyramidal cells [1][2][3]. They are somatostatin-positive, express the nicotinic acetylcholine receptor alpha-2 subunit (Chrna2) as a specific marker in dorsal CA1 [2][8], and play documented roles in theta-band activity, feedback inhibition, and gating of CA3 versus entorhinal cortex inputs to CA1 [7][8]. Mapping OLM cells onto a whole-brain transcriptomic atlas (WMBv1) tests whether this morphologically and operationally defined interneuron class is resolved as a discrete transcriptomic cluster or falls within a larger Sst+ supertype shared with related stratum oriens interneurons.

## Results

**No WMBv1 cluster rises above MODERATE confidence for the canonical OLM definition.** Five candidate atlas clusters were assessed; the primary mapping is 0769 Sst Gaba_3 [CS20230722_CLUS_0769] at MODERATE confidence, representing the best available match at cluster rank within the Sst Gaba_3 supertype. However, the MapMyCells annotation transfer reveals that OLM transcriptomic identity is best captured at the supertype level (0216 Sst Gaba_3) rather than at any single child cluster — at cluster rank, OLM cells disperse across five sibling clusters with CLUS_0768 receiving the most cells.

*The Sst-OLM and Htr3a-OLM Cre-driver subgroups in the Winterer 2019 dataset are pooled as "OLM" in this figure because they are not transcriptomically separable in WMBv1 space. At subclass (053 Sst Gaba) and supertype (0216 Sst Gaba_3) rank, mapping is clean (F1 ~0.97). At cluster rank the signal disperses across child clusters of 0216 Sst Gaba_3, indicating OLM identity is resolved at supertype/subclass rank in WMBv1.*

## Discussion

**Primary mapping:** 0769 Sst Gaba_3 [CS20230722_CLUS_0769] at MODERATE confidence, with the caveat that the AT-supported atlas target within supertype 0216 Sst Gaba_3 is CLUS_0768 rather than CLUS_0769. Key supporting evidence: CA1 SO distribution (87 cells), full Sst/Npy/Pnoc neuropeptide triad, GABA/Sst subclass identity, and convergence of 43/45 OLM cells from Winterer 2019 at the supertype level (F1=0.97 pooled). Key caveats: mGluR1/Grm1 not resolvable from atlas metadata; extra-hippocampal spread into prosubiculum and posterior amygdala indicates CLUS_0769 is broader than the OLM definition; and OLM cells from MapMyCells concentrate on sibling CLUS_0768, not CLUS_0769, at cluster rank. The absence of a transcriptomic distinction between the Sst-OLM and Htr3a-OLM Cre-line subgroups in WMBv1 space indicates the Cre-line split is anatomical/functional rather than transcriptomic at current atlas resolution.

No Cell Ontology term is currently assigned to OLM interneurons. This type meets the criteria for a new CL term request.

### Proposed experiments

1. **Chrna2-Cre + MapMyCells** (highest priority): scRNA-seq from Chrna2-Cre-targeted CA1 stratum oriens neurons followed by MapMyCells onto WMBv1. Target: F1 >= 0.80 at cluster rank against a specific child cluster of 0216 Sst Gaba_3. Expected output: AnnotationTransferEvidence resolving whether OLM cells form a discrete cluster or remain at supertype rank; would also clarify whether CLUS_0768 or CLUS_0769 is the AT-preferred target.
2. **Targeted scRNA-seq or MERFISH of Chrna2+ stratum oriens neurons**: spatial transcriptomics including Grm1 in panel — would quantify mGluR1 at the atlas cluster level and disambiguate CA1 SO OLM cells from the prosubiculum and amygdala components of CLUS_0769.
3. **Patch-seq of Lamp5-Lhx6 neurons in CA3 stratum oriens** (edge to CLUS_0727): would clarify whether OLM-morphology cells fall into the Lamp5 Lhx6 CGE subclass, a biologically surprising assignment currently refuted by AT.

### Open questions

1. Are the CA1 SO cells in CLUS_0769 OLM-morphology cells? What are the prosubiculum (61 cells) and posterior amygdala (95 cells) populations in the same cluster?
2. Why do MapMyCells results preferentially place OLM cells in sibling CLUS_0768 rather than CLUS_0769? Do these two clusters differ in Chrna2 or Grm1 expression, or in hippocampal vs. extra-hippocampal enrichment?
3. Is Sst expression in CLUS_0727 (Lamp5 Lhx6 CGE subclass) biologically meaningful, or are there OLM-morphology cells in the CA3 stratum oriens that unexpectedly fall into a CGE subclass?
4. Are CLUS_0785/0788/0789 (Sst Gaba_6, Chrna2-absent) better interpreted as non-OLM Sst stratum oriens types (e.g. hippocampo-septal, oriens-bistratified, back-projecting)?
5. What accounts for the substantial amygdala component (28%) in CLUS_0789?

## Eliminated candidates

All three UNCERTAIN edges (CLUS_0785, CLUS_0788, CLUS_0789) share the same decisive disqualifying signal: the parent supertype 0219 Sst Gaba_6 is eliminated by ABC Atlas filtering on HPF + GABA + Chrna2 expression [A] — Chrna2, a defining OLM marker, is absent from this supertype. The MapMyCells annotation transfer independently confirms this, with 0/46 OLM cells mapping to Sst Gaba_6. Per-cluster additional caveats: CLUS_0785 also lacks Pnoc (DISCORDANT); CLUS_0789 has a major amygdala component (28% cells) and no CA1 SO signal; CLUS_0788 has only 8 CA1 SO cells with no SLM representation.
