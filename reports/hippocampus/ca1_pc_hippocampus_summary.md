# CA1 pyramidal cell — WMBv1 (CCN20230722) Mapping Report

## Introduction

CA1 pyramidal cells are the principal glutamatergic projection neurons of hippocampal area CA1, constituting the dominant excitatory output of the hippocampal formation and projecting via the subiculum to entorhinal cortex and beyond [2][4]. Glutamatergic neurons dominate hippocampal architecture — accounting for over 90% of all hippocampal neurons — and are densely packed within the pyramidal layer of each Ammon's horn subfield [5].

### 0069 CA1-ProS Glut_1 [CS20230722_SUPT_0069] · 🟡 MODERATE

SUPT_0069 [CS20230722_SUPT_0069] is the highest-scoring WMBv1 supertype candidate for the CA1 pyramidal cell (discovery score 5), belonging to subclass 016 CA1-ProS Glut — the dedicated CA1/prosubiculum glutamatergic subclass in WMBv1. MERFISH data assign 2,553 cells to Field CA1, pyramidal layer [MBA:407], directly matching the classical soma location [UBERON:0014548]. Wfs1, the canonical CA1 pyramidal cell marker, shows mean expression = 3.97 in SUPT_0069 by precomputed stats, supporting molecular correspondence even though it is not a cluster-discriminating marker within the subclass. SUPT_0069 has a total of 13,245 cells in the 10x Chromium reference.

**Supporting evidence:**

- NT type CONSISTENT: SUPT_0069 belongs to subclass 016 CA1-ProS Glut, an exclusively glutamatergic grouping. The TYPE_A_SPLITS relationship is used because WMBv1 resolves at least four CA1-ProS supertypes (SUPT_0069–0072) within this subclass; the classical CA1 pyramidal cell encompasses all of these.

- Soma location CONSISTENT: 2,553 MERFISH cells assigned to Field CA1, pyramidal layer [MBA:407]; MERFISH distribution across stratum oriens [MBA:399] and stratum radiatum [MBA:415] reflects dendritic arborisations of a large supertype.

- Wfs1 expressed: mean expression = 3.97 in precomputed_stats.h5 at the supertype level; not a listed cluster-defining marker within the subclass (listed markers for SUPT_0069: Lefty1, Fibcd1, Pcp4l1).

- Annotation transfer — subclass level: F1=0.995 (group_purity=0.994, target_purity=0.996) from 1,574 CA1-ProS cells to 016 CA1-ProS Glut.

- Annotation transfer — supertype level: F1=0.790 (group_purity=0.654, target_purity=0.999) from 979 CA1-ProS cells to SUPT_0069 [CS20230722_SUPT_0069]. Of 1,704 CA1-ProS cells, 1,011 (59.3%) map to SUPT_0069 at supertype level. Target_purity=1.0 confirms SUPT_0069 is exclusively populated by CA1-ProS cells in this dataset. The remaining CA1-ProS cells distribute across SUPT_0070 (20.1%), SUPT_0072 (13.3%), SUPT_0071 (3.5%), and SUPT_0073 (2.8%), consistent with the TYPE_A_SPLITS relationship.

**Subcluster concordance.** The remaining ~40.7% of CA1-ProS cells distribute across sibling supertypes within subclass 016: 0070 CA1-ProS Glut_2 (20.1%), 0072 CA1-ProS Glut_4 (13.3%), 0071 CA1-ProS Glut_3 (3.5%), and 0073 CA1-ProS Glut_5 (2.8%). This intra-subclass splitting is expected and supports the TYPE_A_SPLITS relationship: all CA1-ProS Glut supertypes within subclass 016 together constitute the atlas representation of the classical CA1 pyramidal cell.

**Concerns:**

- WMBv1 resolves at least four supertypes within subclass 016 (SUPT_0069–0072). SUPT_0069 captures only ~59.3% of CA1-ProS AT-mapped cells; a complete mapping requires additional edges to SUPT_0070, 0071, and 0072.
- Sublayer correspondence of SUPT_0069–0072 to deep vs. superficial CA1 sublayers is unresolved. Wfs1 marks deep-sublayer CA1 PCs in the literature, but which supertype carries the Wfs1-high deep-sublayer signature in the atlas has not been established.

**What would upgrade confidence:** ISH confirmation of a SUPT_0069-specific marker (Lefty1, Fibcd1, or Pcp4l1) in CA1 stratum pyramidale [UBERON:0014548], combined with annotation transfer from a sublayer-resolved CA1 dataset (e.g. Cembrowski 2016 deep vs. superficial), would resolve the primary supertype correspondence and subclass 016 sublayer coverage and could upgrade this mapping to HIGH.

## Discussion

**Primary mapping:** CA1 pyramidal cell → 0069 CA1-ProS Glut_1 [CS20230722_SUPT_0069] at MODERATE confidence under a TYPE_A_SPLITS relationship. Key support: consistent glutamatergic identity, correct MERFISH placement in Field CA1, pyramidal layer (MBA:407, 2,553 cells), confirmed Wfs1 expression (mean 3.97 in precomputed atlas statistics at supertype level), annotation transfer F1=0.790 with target_purity=1.0 (every SUPT_0069-assigned cell originated from the CA1-ProS source label). Key caveats: SUPT_0069 captures only ~59.3% of CA1-ProS AT-mapped cells — the full classical type concept spans all four CA1-ProS Glut supertypes within subclass 016; sublayer correspondence (deep vs. superficial CA1) is unresolved; no CL term is currently assigned.

### Proposed experiments

**Transcriptomics / atlas resolution**

- Run MapMyCells annotation transfer of Cembrowski 2016 or Zeisel 2018 dorsal CA1 pyramidal cell labels onto WMBv1 to resolve SUPT_0069–0072 correspondence and assign each supertype to a CA1 sublayer (deep vs. superficial).
- Cross-check Wfs1 expression levels across SUPT_0069–0072 in precomputed_stats.h5 to identify which supertype carries the Wfs1-high deep-sublayer signature.

**In situ validation**

- ISH or RNAscope for Fibcd1 or Pcp4l1 (SUPT_0069 defining markers) in CA1 stratum pyramidale [UBERON:0014548] to confirm spatial restriction to CA1 pyramidal cells and distinguish from neighboring subtypes.

### Open questions

1. Which CA1-ProS supertypes (SUPT_0069–0072) correspond to deep vs. superficial CA1 pyramidal cell sublayers? Wfs1 marks deep-layer CA1 PCs in the literature; checking which supertype carries Wfs1 in the atlas would resolve the sublayer correspondence.
2. Do supertypes SUPT_0070–0072 represent additional functional or projection-defined CA1 PC subtypes (e.g. deep-projecting vs. lateral entorhinal-projecting)?
3. What CL term structure is appropriate — one term for all CA1 pyramidal cells, or sublayer-specific terms (deep-sublayer vs. superficial-sublayer)?
