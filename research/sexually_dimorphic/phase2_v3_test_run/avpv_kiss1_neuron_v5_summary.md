## Introduction

AVPV/PeN kisspeptin neurons are a sexually dimorphic, female-biased population of
hypothalamic Kiss1+ neurons distributed across the anteroventral periventricular nucleus
[MBA:272] and the posterior periventricular nucleus [MBA:126]. Most of these cells
co-express tyrosine hydroxylase (Th) and oestrogen receptor α (Esr1), and they mediate
oestradiol positive feedback for the preovulatory GnRH/LH surge in females [1][2][3].
They are biologically distinct from the non-dimorphic arcuate (ARC) KNDy population:
greater Kiss1 cell numbers in females than males define the dimorphism [1][3], and the
AVPV/PeN cells are Kiss1-only rather than obligately co-expressing NKB and dynorphin.

## Results

Atlas-metadata marker concordance and an independent bulk-RNA-seq differential
correlation against Stephens 2024 FACS-sorted RP3V vs ARC Kiss1+ pools converge on
the cluster 1915 PVpo-VMPO-MPN Hmx2 Gaba_5 [CS20230722_CLUS_1915] within the supertype
0486 PVpo-VMPO-MPN Hmx2 Gaba_5 [CS20230722_SUPT_0486], at MODERATE confidence (see
property comparison tables and ranked-δ figure below). Cluster-level cell counts are
small (n≈3–5 cells), so the female-bias and expression direction are strong but the
statistical power for the cluster-level call is limited.

**Concerns**

- **Low cell count.** CLUS_1915 contains only n≈3–5 cells; the female-bias direction
  and per-gene means are reliable in sign but statistical power for the cluster-level
  mapping is limited. Confidence is capped at MODERATE pending annotation-transfer
  evidence or larger-dataset replication.
- **Location APPROXIMATE at cluster level.** Only n=1 of the cluster's MERFISH-located
  cells is annotated MBA:272 (AVPV); the primary soma annotation resolves to the broad
  Hypothalamus catchall (MBA:1097, n=3). MERFISH spatial resolution at this cell count
  is insufficient to resolve AVPV vs adjacent PVpo/PeN.
- **Population co-mapping with avpv_th_neuron.** AVPV Th and AVPV Kiss1 cells
  substantially overlap in the classical literature [3][4]; avpv_th_neuron maps to the
  same cluster. CLUS_1915 may be the atlas correlate of the joint Kiss1+Th+ AVPV/PeN
  dopaminergic population rather than the Kiss1-defined subset specifically.
- **No annotation-transfer evidence available.** The transcriptomic support rests on
  cluster-level marker means plus the Stephens 2024 bulk correlation; no MapMyCells F1
  has been computed for this node.

**What would upgrade confidence**

- Larger-cohort transcriptomics of AVPV/PeN Kiss1 neurons to confirm CLUS_1915's
  Kiss1/Th/Esr1+ profile in a sample with adequate cell count.

## Discussion

**Primary mapping:** AVPV/PeN kisspeptin neuron → 1915 PVpo-VMPO-MPN Hmx2 Gaba_5
[CS20230722_CLUS_1915] at MODERATE confidence. Key support: cluster-level Kiss1+Th+Esr1+
co-expression with extreme female bias (MFR=0.02), independently confirmed by the
Stephens 2024 RP3V vs ARC differential bulk correlation (δ=0.090, rank 1 of 5322).
Key caveats: LOW_CELL_COUNT (n≈3–5) and TAXONOMY_LEVEL_MISMATCH (avpv_th_neuron also
maps to CLUS_1915, reflecting the documented Kiss1/Th co-expression in mouse AVPV/PeN
rather than a mapping ambiguity per se).

The Cell Ontology has no specific term for the AVPV/RP3V Kiss1-only (non-KNDy)
population; hypothalamus kisspeptin neuron [[CL:4023123](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/ontologies/cl/classes?obo_id=CL:4023123)]
is the closest ancestor (BROAD). CL:4023123 covers all hypothalamic kisspeptin neurons.
The AVPV/PeN (RP3V) population is Kiss1-only (not obligately KNDy), so the more
specific CL:4023128 (RP3V KNDy neuron) is inappropriate as it requires NKB and
dynorphin co-expression. A dedicated CL term for the AVPV/RP3V Kiss1 non-KNDy
subpopulation does not yet exist — potential CL contribution.
