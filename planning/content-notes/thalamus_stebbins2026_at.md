## Cluster → node correspondence (paper evidence only)

The text assigns **no** neuronal cluster to vLGNe/vLGNi, to a lamina, or to a
projection target. Per-cluster markers exist only in Figure 4 dot plots. The
authors say their clusters don't line up with the Sabbagh 2021 laminae:

> There is a notable disconnect between our identified clusters, which are
> defined by unique combinations of gene expressions, and the single‐gene
> layers described in Sabbagh et al.

They state that Sabbagh's four inhibitory types "were confirmed as distinct
clusters by our integrated analysis" but don't say which clusters.
